Your Privacy & Security in LocalLink

LocalLink connects neighborhood customers, local shopkeepers, and delivery riders in one community marketplace. We request only the minimum permissions required for shopping, store fulfillment, and local delivery tracking.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, email address, phone number, role (Customer, Seller, or Delivery Rider), and securely hashed passwords (`bcrypt`) so your account remains private.
  • Delivery Addresses & Locality: Neighborhood name, street address, and pincode provided by you to filter nearby stores and complete deliveries.
  • Seller & Store Information: Shop name, stall description, product catalog, pricing, and images uploaded intentionally by shop owners.
  • Order Activity: Cart items, placed orders, delivery assignment status, and customer product reviews.

2. Device Permissions (Harmless & Minimal)

  • Approximate & Precise Location (Optional): Used only when you tap GPS location detection to set your delivery neighborhood or when an active Delivery Rider broadcasts live trip coordinates during an assigned delivery.
  • Camera / OS Photo Picker (Optional): Used only when you actively choose to upload a profile picture or product photo. LocalLink does not request broad background storage access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`).
  • Network Access: Used strictly to sync marketplace listings, orders, and live map coordinates over HTTPS.

3. Data Sharing, Protection & Retention

We never sell personal information to third-party advertisers. Delivery addresses and contact numbers are shared solely with the fulfilling merchant and assigned delivery partner for the duration of an order. Authentication tokens are transmitted via standard `Bearer` headers and traffic is protected with TLS/HTTPS in production.

4. Account Deletion & Support

You can sign out at any time from your Account or Rider Profile screen. To request full account or order history deletion, contact the LocalLink support team at support@locallink.app with your registered email address.
