Project Vision & Mission

LocalLink transforms traditional neighborhood street wet-markets, local fishmongers, vegetable vendors, and Kirana stores in Rajpur Sonarpur, Kolkata into a connected digital commerce ecosystem. It cuts out predatory middlemen by giving daily street vendors real-time digital broadcast tools and linking them directly with APMC Sonarpur Mandi Yard wholesale godowns.

Integrated Multi-Tier Ecosystem

1. Customer Marketplace

Hyperlocal 15-minute delivery, handwritten shopping slip AI OCR scanner, custom Bengali fish cuts (Gada, Peti, Ring), multi-stall basket, and live morning stall stories.

2. Retail Merchant Suite

Daily morning fresh catch photo broadcast, Kirana inventory depletion radar, Paytm/PhonePe style voice soundbox alerts, and contactless gate drop-rack bin dispatch.

3. Wholesale Mandi Hub

Direct APMC Yard B2B trading, live godown stock lots, 3-tier wholesale pricing (Standard, Tier 2, Super Bulk), Kirana Khata & Credit book, and tempo gate passes.

4. Autonomous AI Engines

Algorithmic live bargaining engine that defends wholesale floor margins while offering volume discounts, plus multilingual voice synthesizers (English, Bengali, Hindi).
